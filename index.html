📜 How to Use Nephalem's Codex

  1. Open a build guide on Maxroll, Mobalytics, or D4Builds
  2. Set your Build Name and Class — this filters the item list to your class only
  3. Click any Gear Slot to open the editor:
    — Use Quick Pick to browse available items for that slot (filtered by class)
    — Uniques/Mythics: pick an item name — boss source auto-fills in the guide
    — Legendaries: fill in aspect + affixes + tempers + MW priority
  4. Your Acquisition Guide builds live on the right
  5. Hit 📋 Copy to export a plain-text checklist
💾 Auto-saves in your browser between sessions.
🗃️ Database: ~305 unique items across 12 bosses (Season 14, from Owen's loot table).

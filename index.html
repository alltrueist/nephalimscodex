📜 How to Use Nephalem's Codex

  1. Open a build guide on Maxroll, Mobalytics, or D4Builds
  2. Enter your Build Name and Class above
  3. Click each Gear Slot on the left and fill in what the guide shows:
    — For Uniques: just the item name (auto-looks up boss source)
    — For Legendaries: aspect + affixes + tempers + masterwork priority
  4. Your Acquisition Guide updates live on the right
  5. Use the 📋 Copy button to export a text checklist
💾 Your build auto-saves in your browser. It will still be here next time you visit.

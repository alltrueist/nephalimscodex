📜 How to Use Nephalem's Codex

  1. Open a build guide on Maxroll, Mobalytics, or D4Builds
  2. Enter your Build Name and Class
  3. Click each Gear Slot and fill in from your guide:
    — Uniques/Mythics: just enter the item name (boss source auto-fills)
    — Legendaries: enter aspect + affixes + tempers + MW priority
  4. Your Acquisition Guide updates live on the right
  5. Use 📋 Copy to export a plain-text checklist
💾 Your build auto-saves in your browser. It will still be here next time you visit.
